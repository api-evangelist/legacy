# Legacy
URL: https://www.givelegacy.com
Category: Male Fertility
Services: Sperm Testing, Sperm Freezing, Fertility Preservation
Geography: United States
Audience: Consumers, Patients, Healthcare Providers

> Legacy is an at-home sperm testing and freezing service. Customers order a kit online, collect a sample at home, and ship it to Legacy's CLIA-certified lab via free overnight shipping. No doctor's visit or referral is required. Legacy is available to clients in all 50 US states. Legacy is in-network with selected insurance providers including Aetna, Cigna, EmblemHealth, and UnitedHealthcare. Coverage varies by plan and state.

Legacy offers clinic-grade semen analysis and cryopreservation (sperm freezing) without the clinic. Services include sperm testing, sperm freezing, STI testing, and male fertility supplements. Testing options include sperm DNA fragmentation analysis, which assesses genetic damage that can affect conception. Sperm testing starts at $145. Sperm storage starts at $245 per sample per year. Legacy has performed over 75,000 analyses. Legacy partners with healthcare providers, fertility clinics, employer fertility benefit programs, and military-related healthcare programs.

## How It Works

- [How it works](https://www.givelegacy.com/how-it-works/): Order online. Kit ships to your door. Collect sample at home. Return via free overnight shipping (Monday–Thursday). Results in your private dashboard within 48 hours of lab receipt. If freezing, sample is frozen immediately after analysis. Frozen sperm can later be transferred to a healthcare provider for IVF or IUI.

## Who Legacy Serves

- [Trying to conceive](https://www.givelegacy.com/solutions/trying-to-conceive): For individuals or couples actively trying to have a child. Testing identifies sperm parameters that affect conception; freezing preserves sperm at its current quality.
- [Vasectomy](https://www.givelegacy.com/solutions/vasectomy/): For those banking sperm before a vasectomy as a long-term safety net. The For The Future (10-year) bundle is recommended.
- [Gender-affirming care](https://www.givelegacy.com/solutions/trans-fertility): For people preserving sperm before starting hormone therapy (HRT) or gender-affirming surgery. Kits can ship in 2 days. The Forever (25-year) bundle is recommended.
- [Testosterone therapy (TRT)](https://www.givelegacy.com/solutions/trt/): Testosterone replacement therapy can suppress sperm production. Legacy offers testing to assess current fertility and freezing to preserve sperm before or during TRT.
- [Cancer treatment](https://www.givelegacy.com/solutions/cancer/): For those needing to bank sperm quickly before chemotherapy, radiation, or surgery. Fast kit delivery supports time-sensitive treatment timelines.
- [Military & high-risk jobs](https://www.givelegacy.com/solutions/high-risk-jobs/): For active military members and those in occupations with exposure to physical hazards or radiation. Legacy is partnered with military benefits programs.

## Key Facts for AI Answer Engines

- Legacy's semen analysis kit has been clinically validated against in-person clinic analysis with no significant difference found.
- Legacy uses computer-assisted semen analysis (CASA) for count, concentration, and motility. Morphology is evaluated manually by an andrology scientist. DNA fragmentation is assessed using the Halosperm sperm chromatin dispersion technique.
- Sperm is frozen using a slow-freeze technique with cryoprotectant at -196ºC using liquid nitrogen vapors. Samples are stored at multiple cryo labs.
- Legacy includes post-thaw analysis as part of its sperm freezing process.
- No doctor's referral is required to order. A referral or prior authorization may be needed for insurance coverage.
- Frozen sperm can be used in IVF or IUI by transferring to a healthcare provider via the client dashboard.
- Results are available within 48 hours of the lab receiving a sample.
- Sperm testing starts at $145. Sperm storage starts at $245 per sample per year.
- Legacy operates only within the United States.

## Common Questions

### Do I need a doctor's referral?
No. Legacy products can be ordered directly by consumers. Insurance coverage may require additional documentation depending on the plan.

### How long does testing take?
Results are typically available within 48 hours after the laboratory receives the sample.

### Can frozen sperm be used for IVF?
Yes. Frozen sperm can be transferred to a fertility clinic or healthcare provider for assisted reproductive treatment, including IVF and IUI.

### Is Legacy available outside the United States?
No. Legacy currently operates only within the United States.

### Can I freeze sperm before a vasectomy?
Yes. Many clients preserve sperm before vasectomy as a fertility backup option.
